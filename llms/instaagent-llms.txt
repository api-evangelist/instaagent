# InstaAgent

> InstaAgent is a Y Combinator-backed (Spring 2026) AI marketing platform that turns a single
> campaign brief into hundreds of audience-specific creatives. It generates persona-, trend- and
> format-grounded ad variants, distributes them across Meta, TikTok and niche social accounts built
> for specific audiences, tests which personas, hooks, formats and channels drive performance, and
> scales the winners into the next campaign.

InstaAgent Company Limited is operated from a single marketing site at https://instaagent.com/.
Founders: Kyle Wong (Co-Founder & CEO) and Colin Tseung (Co-Founder). The company reports 500+
clients across 10+ countries, 10x ad variants and 30%+ ROI improvement, and $1M annual run rate in
its first ten months.

## Product surface

The product is described in four stages on the public site:

- **Create** — turn one brief into audience-specific creatives grounded in persona, trend and proven format.
- **Distribute** — launch creatives across Meta, TikTok and niche social accounts built for specific audiences.
- **Learn** — test which personas, hooks, formats and channels drive performance.
- **Scale** — scale the winners and make the next campaign smarter.

## No developer API

**InstaAgent publishes no public API.** As of 2026-08-12 there is:

- no OpenAPI, Swagger, GraphQL, AsyncAPI or Protobuf contract at any reachable host;
- no developer portal, API reference, quickstart or SDK — no `api.`, `docs.`, `developer.`,
  `developers.` or `app.` subdomain resolves in DNS;
- no MCP server and no A2A agent card (`/.well-known/agent-card.json` and the legacy
  `/.well-known/agent.json` both 404);
- no GitHub organization (`https://api.github.com/orgs/instaagent` returns 404) and therefore no
  first-party client libraries on npm, PyPI or any other registry;
- no pricing page, no self-serve sign-up and no status page.

Access to the platform is sales-gated: the only entry points are a "Request Demo" form and
`kyle@instaagent.com`. Integrations are with the ad platforms InstaAgent buys on (Meta Ads,
Instagram Ads, TikTok Ads), consumed by InstaAgent — not exposed by it.

## Agent and crawler policy

`https://instaagent.com/robots.txt` carries a Cloudflare-managed Content-Signal of
`search=yes, ai-train=no, use=reference` and disallows CCBot, ClaudeBot,
CloudflareBrowserRenderingCrawler, Google-Extended, GPTBot and meta-externalagent. The site permits
search indexing and reference use, but refuses AI training.
